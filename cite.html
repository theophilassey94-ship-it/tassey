About CITE

Building Skills for a Better Future

Center for International Training and Empowerment (CITE) is an indigenous Tanzanian organization registered under the Company's Act of 2002 with registration number 531194. Based in Arusha, we are committed to leveraging intellectual capital to meet the growing needs of modern organizations.

We provide standard and tailor-made courses designed to address the challenges faced by modern organizations due to globalization and rapidly evolving technology.

Our Mission

To facilitate a conducive learning environment that promotes professional and non-professional knowledge enhancement through tailor-made and standard courses to pursue the full potential of the workforce in modern organizations.

Our Vision

To offer world-class professional and non-professional trainings to individuals and firms to increase efficiency and productivity, as well as Youth empowerment for a brighter future.
